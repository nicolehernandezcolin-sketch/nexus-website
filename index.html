The Night

A room made of light and fabric

Behind The Curtain turns an ordinary space into something you step into. Sheer curtains hang through the room and become screens: film, faces, and words are projected across the fabric while performers move behind and through it. Haze hangs in the air, candles light the corners, and the music carries the night from the first set to the last.

What We're Building With UCSD

Chapter 01 starts here

Nexus is partnering with the UC San Diego community to build a home for student culture: a night where artists, clubs, and audiences who usually stay in their own circles end up in the same room.

01
A Stage for Student Artists
Real production and a real audience. UCSD musicians, DJs, dancers, and visual artists perform inside a fully designed experience.
02
Every Club, One Room
Music, dance, film, art, and cultural orgs come together to shape the night and bring their communities with them.
03
The Start of Something
Behind The Curtain is the first chapter of the Nexus UC Series, a campus series built to come back and grow with UCSD.

03
SMRITHI SURESH
Bharatanatyam · Indian Classical · Choreographer · San Diego

Smrithi Suresh has trained in Bharatanatyam for 18 years — a lifelong love that began with a single step learned from her aunt. She has studied under Gurus Smt. Suganda Sreenath, Smt. Shreya Iyer of Jayendra Kalenkendra, and later Smt. Radhika Karandikar in San Diego. The form has taken her to stages in Dallas, the Bay Area, Cleveland, New York, and India, and earned her recognition as an IndianRaga Fellow and performer at TedXBerkeley and the Erasing Borders Dance Festival. Dance, for Smrithi, is both a bridge to her cultural heritage and a connection to all art forms and people. In 2021 she co-founded Fuchsia Dance — a space for Indian classical dancers to collaborate and push the boundaries of tradition. Most recently she participated in the inaugural AADF dance residency in Oceanside.

@smrithi.sur @fuchsiadance fuchsiadance.org

THE RANGERS
Hip-Hop · Street · Collective · Multi-Studio

The Rangers are a group of individuals from many places — coming together from Culture Shock, Studio FX, Syde FX, 220 Family, high schools, and the LA industry. Their goal is to spread the overwhelming feeling of love for dance and artistry.

About
Nexus

Nexus is a San Diego cultural organization at the intersection of music, dance, and film. We create live experiences, build community, and give independent artists the stage they deserve.

What We Do

The
Mission

San Diego is full of artists doing remarkable work, and too few people outside the city know it. Nexus exists to change that. We bring artists together across music, dance, and film, create nights worth remembering, and build the spaces and relationships a creative city needs to thrive.

Every event we produce, every artist we feature, and every series we build points toward the same goal: a San Diego the world pays attention to.
