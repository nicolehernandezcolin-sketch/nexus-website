Nexus  ·  Mel's Room  ✦

Mel's
Room

DJ sets in places you wouldn't expect. A rooftop, a bookstore, a parking garage, a garden at midnight — wherever the music feels like it shouldn't be, that's exactly where Mel's Room shows up.

The idea is simple: music sounds different when the room surprises you. Mel's Room moves every month — a new location, a new mood, a new crowd. The only thing that stays the same is the quality of the set and the feeling that you found something nobody else knows about yet.

The Location
Secret until 24 hours before. Ticket holders get the address direct. Walk in not knowing what the room looks like — that's the point.
The Sound
No genre rules. The DJ decides what the space needs. Sets have gone deep house, Afrobeats, soul, broken beat, jazz fusion — the music follows the room.
The Crowd
Intentionally small. 40–80 people. No massive venues, no anonymous nights. Everyone in the room found out the same way — they were paying attention.
The Vibe
No VIP, no bottle service, no dress code beyond showing up as yourself. The music is the main character. Everything else is set dressing.

In 2021 she co-founded Fuchsia Dance — a space for Indian classical dancers to collaborate and push the boundaries of tradition. About
Nexus

Nexus is a San Diego–based cultural organization at the intersection of music, dance, film, fashion, and sport. We create events, build community, and give artists the stage they deserve — without boundaries between disciplines.

What We Do

The
Mission

San Diego has one of the most vibrant creative communities in the country — and one of the most underrepresented on the national stage. Nexus exists to change that. We bring artists together across every discipline, create moments that matter, and build the infrastructure for a thriving cultural ecosystem in this city.

Every event we produce. Every artist we feature. Every series we build. It all points toward the same thing: a San Diego that the world pays attention to.
