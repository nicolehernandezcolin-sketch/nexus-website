Where the money goes

Every online and at-the-door donation, and every toy, supports families separated by ICE and affected by immigration policies in San Diego.

$10 donationsCollected online through Nexus (shown live above) and in cash at the door. Nexus then redistributes the full amount directly to affected families.
Community toy driveOne toy per participant, gathered at the event and given to children in affected families this holiday season.
Processing feeThe $1.29 added online covers card processing, so the full $10 goes to families.
Online total = the "Donations" figure above. Door cash and toy counts are added by the organizers after the event.
