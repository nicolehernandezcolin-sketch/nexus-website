How this works

Every number here comes from the same place the tickets do. When someone taps Get Tickets, the Nexus ticket server records an order, then opens a Stripe checkout for that exact order. When Stripe takes the money it tells our server, and the order turns paid and the buyer's QR tickets are issued.

  1. Visit – someone lands on nexusglobal.info (we note where they came from: Instagram, Linktree, a typed link…).
  2. Event page & ticket page – they look at the event, then the ticket card.
  3. Get Tickets – they open the box office and enter their name, email and phone.
  4. Payment – Stripe's secure checkout opens inside our page.
  5. Paid – Stripe confirms; tickets are issued and counted here.

Money: buyers pay the ticket price plus the Nexus fee (3% + $0.99 per ticket). Stripe's processing (about 2.9% + $0.30 per order) comes out of what Nexus receives, so "Est. to Nexus" is gross minus that estimate. Payouts land in the bank account connected to the Nexus Stripe account.

Privacy: visitor tracking is anonymous (a random ID, no names). Buyer names and emails are shortened on this page so it can be shared.
